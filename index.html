METHODOLOGY

Data, definitions
& limitations.

Transparent summaries make better decisions. Here’s what the dashboard includes and how to read it.

01

Dataset coverage

100,000 vehicle records, 37 fields, model years spanning 2018–2026, and 200 manufacturers represented in the supplied extract.

02

Analysis dimensions

Vehicle specifications, battery technology, charging, ownership costs, sales, geography, ratings and estimated environmental impact.

03

Interpretation

Summary figures describe the supplied dataset. They are not independently verified real-world market statistics; sales totals reflect the recorded annual sales field.
